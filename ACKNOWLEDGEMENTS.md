# Acknowledgements

The idea comes from [blast-radius](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code-playground/tree/main/claude-code/mods/blast-radius), a mod in Anthropic's claude-code-playground that holds a risky Bash command and shows what it would change before it runs. impact-radius is written from scratch: it also watches PowerShell and measures without bash so it works on Windows, holds third-party installs for the whole user or system, and speaks English and Spanish.
